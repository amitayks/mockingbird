The movement

Ethereal is a refusal of weight.

It began as a reaction against the flat, boxed severity of the interface era — a wish to make software feel like standing in dawn light rather than reading a spreadsheet. It borrows from luminism, from cathedral glass, from the way fog holds a sunrise.

Feel

Serenity

The viewer should exhale. No urgency in the motion, no sharpness in the light.

Craft

Luminance

Hierarchy is built from brightness and blur — the important thing simply glows more.

Ethos

Weightlessness

Everything floats a little. Gravity is the one force Ethereal quietly ignores.
